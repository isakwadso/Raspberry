Kalibrera för sensorn:
The DS18B20 is only specified to ±0.5 °C absolute, so 60.0 on the screen can be up to half a degree off the true temperature. 
Your requirement is 0.1 °C at the outlet, so while you calibrate the volumes, also measure the temperature of the liquid 
at the tip with a reference thermometer. 
The difference goes into the sensor offset in settings.json (sensor_offset_k).
